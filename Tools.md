# Free Tools List (Updated November 2026)

## Script & Ideas
- Gemini (Free) – Recommended
- ChatGPT Free
- Any free model via OpenRouter or similar

## Text-to-Speech
- CapCut built-in TTS (easiest)
- Edge-TTS (high quality, free)
- Voicebox / Kokoro (local, free)

## Image Generation
- Gemini Image
- Dreamina
- Free tiers of Flux / other image tools

## Video Generation (Free Credits)
| Tool          | Free Allowance              | Notes                     |
|---------------|-----------------------------|---------------------------|
| Google Flow   | \~50 credits per day         | Currently one of the best |
| PixVerse      | 30 credits per day          | Good for stylized clips   |
| Kling         | Daily login credits         | Strong motion             |
| Dreamina      | Daily credits               | Good inside CapCut        |
| CapCut AI     | Limited free generations    | Convenient                |

## Editing
- CapCut (Mobile + Desktop) – Strongly recommended
- Clipchamp (Free)

## Notes
- Free credits usually reset daily and do not roll over.
- Always generate the most important shots first.
- You can mix clips from different tools in one video.
