# Sample Script – "The Bridge That Shouldn't Have Fallen"

**Total length target:** \~62 seconds

**Shot 1** (0–4s)
Narration: Seventy-four soldiers walked across a bridge… and it collapsed beneath them.
Visual: Wide shot of an old iron bridge. Soldiers marching in formation. Dramatic lighting.

**Shot 2** (4–9s)
Narration: The year was 1831. The place: Broughton, England.
Visual: Establishing shot of a small town and the bridge. Slight camera push-in.

**Shot 3** (9–16s)
Narration: The bridge was made of iron. Strong. Modern for its time. Everyone trusted it.
Visual: Close details of the iron structure. Hands touching the metal.

**Shot 4** (16–24s)
Narration: But the soldiers marched in perfect step. Left. Right. Left. Right.
Visual: Low angle of boots hitting the bridge in unison. Rhythmic.

**Shot 5** (24–32s)
Narration: The bridge began to shake. Then it began to bounce.
Visual: The bridge vibrating. Soldiers looking at each other.

**Shot 6** (32–40s)
Narration: And then… it snapped.
Visual: Dramatic collapse. Slow motion fragments.

**Shot 7** (40–50s)
Narration: The reason was simple. Resonance. The same force that can shatter a glass with sound.
Visual: Simple analogy visual (glass vibrating) mixed with the bridge.

**Shot 8** (50–62s)
Narration: After that day, soldiers were ordered to break step when crossing bridges. A rule that still exists.
Visual: Modern soldiers crossing a bridge, deliberately out of step. Text fade or clean ending frame.
