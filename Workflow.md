# Workflow – Free AI Shorts Pipeline

## Goal
Create a 50–70 second vertical short (9:16) with narration, simple animation/motion, captions, and background music.

---

### Step 1 – Write the Script (5–10 min)
1. Open `prompts/script-prompt.md`
2. Replace `[TOPIC]` with your idea
3. Paste into Gemini (recommended) or any free LLM
4. Copy the final script

Optional: Ask the AI to also give a short visual description for each shot.

---

### Step 2 – Generate Voiceover
Options (all free):
- CapCut built-in Text-to-Speech (easiest)
- Edge-TTS (good quality, many voices)
- Kling / PixVerse built-in voice (if available)

Save the audio file.

---

### Step 3 – Create Key Visuals
For the most important shots (hook, climax, ending):
1. Use `prompts/image-prompt.md`
2. Generate images with Gemini, Dreamina, or any free image tool
3. Keep character and style consistent when possible

---

### Step 4 – Turn Images / Text into Video Clips
Use free daily credits from:
- Google Flow (best quality among free options)
- PixVerse
- Kling
- Dreamina / CapCut Seedance

Tips:
- Generate 3–6 second clips
- Prioritize the hook and the strongest moments first
- You can mix different tools

---

### Step 5 – Edit in CapCut
1. Create a new 9:16 project
2. Put the clips in order
3. Add the voiceover and sync it
4. Add auto captions (word-by-word looks best)
5. Add light background music (lower the volume)
6. Export in 1080p

---

### Step 6 – Optional Improvements
- Add simple zoom/pan on still images if you don’t have enough video clips
- Use CapCut effects for transitions
- Check loudness so voice is clearly above music

---

## Recommended Daily Routine
1. Write 1–2 scripts in the morning
2. Generate the most important clips with free credits
3. Finish editing in the evening
